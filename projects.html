Fabflix (Movie Search website)

June 2025

  • Languages used: JavaScript, HTML, CSS, Java, SQL
  • Tools & Platforms: Maven, Apache Tomcat, Docker, AWS, Kubernetes, Google reCAPTCHA, https
  • Users can search by title, director’s name, star’s name, or year as well as by letter or by genre
  • Search results can be organized by the preference of the user based on movie rating and/or title
  • Users must log in and can add movies to their shopping cart and make purchases
